# Downstream

A TRMNL recipe that shows where a raindrop falling at your location ends up: the chain of streams and rivers it follows, how far it travels, and the sea or lake where it ends.

**Status:** in setup, not yet usable. See `PROJECT.md`.

## Data and attribution

- River network and sub-basins: HydroSHEDS (HydroRIVERS, HydroBASINS). _Full attribution statement and citation (Lehner & Grill 2013) to be added verbatim from the HydroSHEDS license agreement once re-fetched (R17)._
- River names, lakes and sea areas: Made with Natural Earth (public domain).
- Basemap: © OpenStreetMap contributors.

Engineered drainage (dams, canals, city sewers) is not modeled.
